#### Variation NC001526 nucleotide position 4042

The nucleotide position 4042 of the HPV16 E5 gene is highly polymorphic and leads to  different amino acid substitutions at position 65 (I65) of the viral protein. 
The variability of position 4042 differs among the HPV16 variant lineages, since in the A and D variant lineages is found as A4042G (I65V), in C variant lineage is detected as A4042G/C (I65V/L) and in B lineage it is addressed as A4042G/T (I65V) ([1](),[2]()). 
Research studies have revealed that the respective mutation is exclusively detected in advanced cervical dysplasia and cervical cancer cases ([1](), [2]()).
Moreover, it has been proposed that the amino acid variability at position 65 of E5 protein is associated with viral persistence and increased viral tumorigenicity ([1]()).
