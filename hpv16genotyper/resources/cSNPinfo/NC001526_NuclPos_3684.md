#### Variation NC001526 nucleotide position 3684

The nucleotide mutation C3684A of the E2 gene induces the amino acid substitution T310K that is positioned in the DNA binding domain of the HPV16 E2 protein. 
A recent study has suggested that the T310K is associated with cervical cancer risk in Tunisian patients. 
The amino acid mutation T310K might constraint the E2 function as transcription suppressor, thus influencing the expression of viral oncogenes E6 and E7 ([1]()).  
The nucleotide variation C2860A results to the amino acid substitution H35Q which is positioned in the transactivation domain of the E2 protein.
The H35Q was found to be strongly associated with cervical cancer development in Han Chinese women ([2]()). 
The mechanism that H35Q influences the function of the E2 protein is required to be further investigated.
