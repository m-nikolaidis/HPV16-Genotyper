#### Variation NC001526 nucleotide position 3410

The nucleotide variation C3410T that is detected in HPV16 E2 gene leads to the amino acid substitution P219S in the hinge region of viral protein. 
The P219S is extensively distributed among all HPV16 variant lineages and it has been strongly associated with severe cervical dysplasia and cervical cancer development ([1](#citations)-[4](#citations)). 
It has been anticipated that the tumorigenic ability of P219S is emerged due to structural changes that it induces in the hinge region of the E2 protein. 
In particular, it has been proposed that the P219S may change the three-dimensional structure of E2 protein, thus modifying the link between N-terminal transactivation domain and DNA binding domain ([1]()-[4]()).
