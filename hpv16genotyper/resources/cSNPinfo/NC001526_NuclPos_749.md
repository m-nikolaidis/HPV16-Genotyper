#### Variation NC001526 nucleotide position 749

The nucleotide variation C749T of the HPV16 E7 gene induces the amino acid change S63F in the E7 oncoprotein. 
A recent analysis in Han Chinese population revealed that there is strong relationship between S63F and cervical cancer risk ([1]()). 
The distribution of S63F differs among studies including 14.3% for Sichuan in central China ([2]()) 59.5 % for Korea ([3]()), 0.9 % for Germany ([4]()) and 36.4 % for Tanzania ([5]())
