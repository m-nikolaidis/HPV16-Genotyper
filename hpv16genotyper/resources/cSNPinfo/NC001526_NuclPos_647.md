#### Variation NC001526 nucleotide position 647

The nucleotide variation A647G of the HPV16 E7 gene induces the amino acid change N29S. Recent studies have revealed that the N29S is more frequently detected in HSILs and cervical cancer incidences in patients from Korea, China and Shanghai ([1]()-[4]()).
