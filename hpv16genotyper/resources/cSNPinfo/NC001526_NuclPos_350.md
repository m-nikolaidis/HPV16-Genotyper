#### Variation NC001526 nucleotide position 350
The nucleotide variation T350G leads to the amino acid substitution L83V in the E6 oncoprotein ([1](#citations)-[5](#citations)). The position 350 has been found to be polymorphic T/G in HPV16 A lineage, monomorphic T in HPV16 B and C lineages and monomorphic G in HPV16 D lineage.  
The E6 oncoprotein with L83V facilitates the transformation of human keratinocytes more effectively than prototype E6 oncogene and changes the expression profile of cervical carcinoma C33A cells . The L83V has been associated with severe dysplasia and cervical cancer development in several geographic areas including Central/South America and various countries in Europe ([5]()-[9]()).
