#### Variation NC001526 nucleotide position 2860

The nucleotide variation C2860A results to the amino acid substitution H35Q which is positioned in the transactivation domain of the E2 protein. 
The H35Q was found to be strongly associated with cervical cancer development in Han Chinese women ([1]()).
The mechanism that H35Q influences the function of the E2 protein is required to be further investigated.
