#### Variation NC001526 nucleotide position 145

The nucleotide variations G145T and C335T of the HPV16 E6 gene lead to the amino acid substitution Q14H, and H78Y, respectively. Both sequence mutations are detected in D variant lineage. 
It has been proposed that Q14H, H78Y along with L83V provide E6 oncoprotein a significant selective advantage in immortalization and transformation capacity over the prototype viral oncoprotein ([1](),[2]()).
